About

Built around accuracy, not guesswork.

Renderonomy exists because most product renders look close, but not quite right. Proportions slightly off. Materials that don't match. Details that read like a guess instead of the real thing.

Every project starts from your actual product, whether that's a CAD file, a spec sheet, or clear reference photos, and pairs that with a network of contracted 3D artists who handle the render work. One consistent standard applied to every project, no bloated agency overhead sitting between you and the work.

Renderonomy applies a mechanical engineering eye for CAD accuracy to every project, with direct, responsive communication throughout, not a queue behind an account manager.

Process

Four steps, in order. Nothing skipped.

01

Spec

Send your CAD file, reference photos, or drawings. Whatever you've got is a fine starting point.

02

Model

We build or verify an accurate 3D model, checked against your product before anything gets rendered.

03

Render

Studio shots, lifestyle scenes, or 360° turntables, all pulled from the same accurate model.

04

Deliver

Files sized and formatted for your store, ads, or campaign page. Ready to use.

Turnaround

What to expect along the way.

Timelines depend on how complex the product is and how many angles you need, so you'll get a real estimate after step one instead of a generic promise up front. You'll see the model before it's rendered, so anything off gets caught early instead of after the final images are done.

FAQ

Common questions.

What file formats do you accept?

CAD files (STEP, STL, OBJ, and similar) are ideal. No CAD file yet? Clear reference photos from a few angles work as a starting point instead.

How long does a render take?

Depends on how complex the product is and how many angles or scenes you need. You'll get a real estimate after the first look at your product, not a generic promise up front.

How does pricing work?

Quoted per project once we know what's involved. No fixed public pricing since it varies by product complexity and how many renders you need.

What if I need changes after the first render?

Bring it up during the project, revisions get worked out on a case-by-case basis depending on what's changing.

What industries do you work with?

Precision hardware, electronics, cosmetics, fitness equipment, furniture, and campaign-stage products, see the homepage for the full range and some real examples.
