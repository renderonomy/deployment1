How a project runs.

You see a draft before anything gets rendered at full quality, so changes happen early and cheaply.

  1. Send what you have

    CAD files, drawings, or photos from a few angles. Rough dimensions help when there's no CAD.

  2. Get a fixed quote

    Price and delivery date agreed before work starts. Usually back to you within a day.

  3. Approve a draft

    A quick preview of angles, materials, and lighting. Tell us what to change.

  4. Final renders

    Full resolution, typically 2 to 4 business days for a single product.

  5. Revise and deliver

    Two rounds of revisions included. PNG or JPG, transparent backgrounds on request.

A few things we hold to.

No generative AI in final images

Every pixel comes from a 3D model of your product, rendered with real materials and lighting.

No production CAD

We read your engineering files to get geometry right. We don't make manufacturing drawings.

Your files stay private

Pre-launch products are normal for us. We're happy to sign your NDA before you send anything.

Questions people ask first.

What files can you work from?

STEP, IGES, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, STL, OBJ, and FBX all work. PDF drawings and photos are fine too, we just build the model from them first.

I don't have CAD yet. Can you still render it?

Yes. Send clear photos from a few angles and rough dimensions, and we model it from those. It adds a little time, which the quote will reflect.

How long does it take?

A single product is usually 2 to 4 business days after you approve the draft. Larger lines and scenes take longer, and you'll get a delivery date with the quote.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds are included. Most changes get caught at the draft stage, before final rendering.

Who owns the images?

You do, once the project is paid. Use them anywhere: your store, ads, Kickstarter, packaging, press.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send it over with your first message and we'll sign before you share files.

Do you use AI to make the images?

No. The images are rendered from a 3D model of your product. That's the reason they match the real thing.

Tell us about your product.

What it is, what files you have, and what you need the images for. You'll get a fixed quote back, usually within a day.
